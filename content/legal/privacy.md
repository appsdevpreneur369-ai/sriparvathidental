This policy explains how Sri Parvathi Dental Clinic, First Floor, Perreddy Complex, Pathur Road Cross, Tadepalle, Andhra Pradesh 522501 ("the clinic", "we") handles your personal data when you use this website or book an appointment. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## What we collect

- **Booking details:** your name, mobile number, email address, the reason for your visit and the time you choose.
- **Health information** you share with us at or after your visit, recorded in your patient file.
- **Technical data** needed to run the website securely, such as your browser type and IP address in server logs.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

## Why we use it

- To book, confirm, remind you of and manage your appointments.
- To provide dental care and keep your treatment records, prescriptions and invoices.
- To contact you about your appointment by SMS, WhatsApp, phone or email, as you agreed when booking.
- To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We never sell your data.

## Who processes it

Appointments, patient records and reminders are managed on **ClinicFlow247**, our clinic software provider, which processes data on our behalf and stores it securely. *(Data-storage location to be confirmed with ClinicFlow247 before publishing.)* Messages are sent through the provider's SMS, WhatsApp and email services.

## How long we keep it

Clinical records are kept for as long as the law and professional guidelines require. Booking requests that never became an appointment are deleted after a reasonable period.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent for future processing, and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
