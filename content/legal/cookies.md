This website does **not** use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

## What the site stores in your browser

- **Session storage** remembers, for this browser tab only, whether you have already seen or closed the booking pop-up, so it does not appear again. It is cleared when you close the tab.

## Third-party content

- The **Google Map** on the contact page loads only when you choose to show it. Google may then set its own cookies under Google's privacy policy.
- **Patient sign-in and booking pages** on ClinicFlow247 may use cookies or browser storage that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in.

## Server logs

Like most websites, the server that hosts this site keeps short-lived technical logs (such as the page requested, time and IP address) to keep it secure and working. These are not used to profile visitors.

## Controlling storage

You can clear or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. The website will still work, though the booking pop-up may appear again.
