## Medicines and home remedies

Any mention of painkillers, mouthwashes or home care is general guidance. Always follow the instructions on the pack, and check with a doctor or pharmacist before taking a new medicine — especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking other medicines or have a medical condition.

## Urgent problems

If you have severe pain, swelling, bleeding that will not stop, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or an injury to the face or jaw, seek urgent care — see [dental emergencies](/emergency).
